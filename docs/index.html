Pocket Stable

Pony Life

Raise one-of-a-kind ponies: feed, groom and ride them, and grow your stable!

Now in open betaJoin early on Google Play

Pocket Stable is a little stable that lives in your pocket. Every pony you welcome is one of a kind: its own coat and markings, mane and tail, eyes and name, unlike any other.

Your ponies live in real time. They get hungry while you're away, doze off at night, and are glad to see you when you come back.

Look after them

  • Feed them carrots when they're hungry, but not too many… an overfed pony may not stay a pony for long!
  • Wash and brush them, clean up after them, and stroke them until they're happy.
  • Ask how they feel: your pony will find a way to tell you, in its own words.
  • A gentle reminder lets you know when someone is getting hungry (only during the day).
  • On your first day, Sophia shows you around the stable and helps you get started.

Ride them

Once your stable grows, the riding school opens. Sophia's lessons teach you to trot, gallop, jump and duck, then each pony follows its own path, one track after another. Ride fast enough and the next track opens, and every track has stars to earn. Even foals can join in!

Watch them grow

  • Every pony starts as a wobbly foal, grows into a yearling, and becomes an adult.
  • Day turns to night over the paddock, and every pony keeps its own bedtime.
  1. Foal
  2. Yearling
  3. Grown

Build your stable

The better you care for your ponies, the more your stable grows: climb the ranks, celebrate every level, and unlock room for new ponies.

Every pony is one of a kind

A real bond

Your ponies depend on you. Forget one for too long and it will leave the stable for good, so pop in often, even just for a cuddle.

No account required, no connection needed: your stable lives on your phone.
